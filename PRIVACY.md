# Privacy Policy for Lume

**Last updated:** September 2026

Lume ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a minimalist new tab extension created by Praise Ifenkwe. We believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Lume is engineered from the ground up to keep your personal data strictly on your own device.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Analytics
- We do **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
- We do **not** use any analytics platforms, tracking cookies, user fingerprinting, or behavioral telemetry.

## 2. Permissions & How They Are Used
Lume requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver its core features:

- **`storage`**: Used exclusively on your local device to remember your settings (such as your preferred clock format, selected wallpaper theme, ambient audio volume, and custom dock bookmarks) via `chrome.storage.local`.
- **`geolocation`**: Used only when you choose to display local weather. Coordinates are used solely to query the Open-Meteo weather API and are never logged, stored on external servers, or shared with third parties.
- **`bookmarks` & `topSites`**: Used strictly within the extension UI to display your bookmarks or top visited sites in the dock. Your browsing history is never transmitted anywhere.
- **`favicon`**: Used strictly to render website icons next to your bookmarks in the dock.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Lume connects directly from your browser to the following privacy-respecting public APIs:
- **Open-Meteo**: For real-time weather forecasts (does not require or store personal data).
- **Unsplash**: For retrieving rotating high-resolution photography wallpapers.
- **IP-API / BigDataCloud**: Optional fallback to estimate coarse city-level location for weather only when browser geolocation is unavailable.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
Any future updates to this policy will be reflected in this repository. Because Lume collects zero user data, significant policy changes are not anticipated.
